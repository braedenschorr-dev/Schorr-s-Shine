Good to Know

Questions people usually ask

Do I need to bring my car anywhere?
No — that's the whole point. We come to your home, office, or gym with everything we need. No drop-offs, no waiting rooms.
Do I need to provide water or power?
Yes — we ask for access to a water spigot and a power outlet at your location. We bring everything else: all professional-grade tools, products, and supplies.
How long does a detail take?
Most interior details take about 2 hours on average, depending on your vehicle's size and condition.
What if I'm not happy with the results?
We'll make it right — no questions, no hassle. That's our satisfaction guarantee.
How fast will I hear back after requesting a quote?
Usually within a few hours. Prefer to skip the form? Call or text us directly at (518) 543-7564.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Venmo, PayPal, and Cash App.
